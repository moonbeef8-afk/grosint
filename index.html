FIELD MANUAL // SAFE & REPEATABLE WORKFLOW

Investigation workflow

01 — Define the question

Write down exactly what needs verifying. Avoid starting with an assumption about who someone is.

02 — Collect public-source leads

Use the search modules and keep the original source URL, date, and context.

03 — Corroborate independently

Seek a second independent source. A username match, IP location, or caller-ID label alone is not proof of identity.

04 — Preserve evidence

Keep original screenshots and messages. Record UTC/local timestamps and calculate hashes where useful.

05 — Escalate safely

For threats, extortion, or immediate danger, report evidence to the platform and police. Do not confront or publicly expose a suspected person.
